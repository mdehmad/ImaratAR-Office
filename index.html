Augmented Reality

See it on site.

Stand in the grey structure, mark your start point, and the full interior appears around you at real size.

1

Choose a start point

In the 3D view, tap "Set start point" on a spot you can find on site: the entrance, a door or a column corner.

2

Stand on it

On site, stand at that spot and tap Start AR. Allow camera access.

3

See it at full scale

Scan the floor slowly. The office appears at 1:1 on the ring. Aim the ring at your start point and tap.

4

Line it up

Turn it with ⟲ ⟳ until the walls match the real ones, then walk through.

Launch AR Experience
Android: live AR in Chrome. iPhone / iPad: opens in Apple AR, where you drag to move and twist to rotate.
